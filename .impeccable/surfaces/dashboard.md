# TracePilot operational workspace
Mode: Operate. Scope: application shell, dashboard, shared controls, incident views and theme consistency throughout management, reports and settings.
Audience: internal responders managing incidents across customer projects.
Confirmed goal: faster incident triage and clearer priorities. Preserve Dutch copy, product truth, routes, reporting, forms, native controls, blue/navy brand, style.md and dark mode.
Build path: code-led; the existing style guide pins the visual world and the work concerns operational hierarchy and actual interactive states.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Put the work queue at the centre of the workspace; urgency and the next action are readable before analysis.
OWN-WORLD: Inter, navy text, blue selection, white navigation, cool page background, restrained 14px surfaces; red, amber and green communicate operational states. Existing brand assets remain.
STORY: Responders scan global open/handling/completed counts, see SLA attention, filter the queue, and open an incident to complete its next step.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact persistent toolbar and grouped sidebar; greeting plus registration action; three consistent status summaries; conditional SLA attention; a full-width queue with a separate filter row. Incident title and project share a column. Supporting priority distribution is below the queue and explicitly limited to the current page. On mobile: greeting, SLA attention, queue with expandable filters, then status summaries.
FORM: Familiar operational workspace, pinned by style.md; signature move is the priority rhythm in the queue: severity first, incident context second, SLA and progress alongside. State feedback uses restrained colour transitions, never page-load choreography. Seed: pinned-tracepilot-triage.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
